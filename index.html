Description

Parol Catcher is a fast reaction game inspired by Philippine Christmas lanterns. Parols light up at random on a 3×3 board and vanish quickly. Catch as many gold parols as you can in 30 seconds. It runs in any browser, on phones by touch and on computers by mouse or keyboard.

Mechanics

  1. Press Start. A 30-second timer begins.
  2. A parol lights up in a random square and stays for a short time.
  3. Tap or click a gold parol to earn 1 point.
  4. Tap a red parol and you lose 2 points. The score never drops below 0.
  5. Parols appear faster as the time runs down.
  6. When time is up, your score is shown. Your best score is kept until you close the page.

Keyboard: use Tab to move between squares and Enter or Space to tap.
